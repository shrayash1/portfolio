Pocket Wilds

A pixel monster meadow. Pick a fire, water or grass partner, learn moves, evolve twice and survive ten minutes of wild monsters.

How to play

MouseClick the game to lock the mouse, then move it to turn the camera. W A S DMove. Forward is wherever the camera faces. RUse your ultimate when the circle is full. Defeating monsters fills it. CoinsMonsters drop experience or a wild coin. Spend coins on chests (E): each gives a boost item for the run (Common to Legendary) or a move scroll, and each chest costs more than the last. A few blue chests are free, and bosses leave one. TabOpen the meadow map. In the level-up screen, F rerolls the picks and X skips them (twice per run each). SpaceJump. High enough and you clear ground monsters and low shots. ShiftDash a short way in the direction you are moving. You can't be hit while dashing. EInteract. At a boss altar (red crystal) it calls that boss, whose chest is better. At a chest it opens it. Each works once per run. Bosses slam, charge and fire volleys, so watch for the red circle. MTurn sound on or off. EscPause and open the menu. Press again to resume. PhoneThe game goes full screen when a run starts (turn the phone sideways). Left thumb moves and the camera follows, right thumb looks around, the circle fires your ultimate, E calls a boss and ⛶ toggles full screen. 1 2 3Pick a reward when you level up.

Your moves fire on their own. Walk over the glowing orbs to gain experience and pick a new move or trait each level. Your partner only learns moves of its own type. You heal slowly when you avoid damage for a few seconds.

Radar. The circle at the top right shows monsters (coloured by type), bosses (big red dots), crates (brown) and items (gold). The gold ring is the fence at the edge of the meadow.

Crates. Break them by attacking or walking into them for coins, a Heal Berry, a Magnet (pulls in every orb), a Bomb (blasts everything nearby) or a Shield (6 seconds of safety).

Your partner evolves at level 12 and level 27, recruits included. A boss shows up every 2:00. Beat a boss once and it joins you as a playable partner, including Boulderback, the ground-type boss.

Abilities and status effects. Every partner has its own ability, like Overheat for the fire starter. Moves can also leave a status on monsters: fire burns, water chills (chill a monster three times and it freezes solid), grass poisons and ground dazes. Open the Wild Dex to read every partner's ability, stats and moves.

Recruits. Each monster has a challenge (see the Wild Dex), such as defeating 150 of them, freezing 80 monsters or breaking 40 stones. Progress is kept between runs. Finish it and, in a later run, the monster may roam the map (one per run, more often the commoner it is), with an arrow pointing to it. It only fights if you get close, then runs. Bring it below a quarter of its HP and stay in its ring for 12 seconds; leave for 5 seconds and it resets. Bosses are Legendary: their challenges are harder, and capturing one adds a Legendary power-up to every partner's level-up picks.

The world. Seven regions, each with its own look and favourite monsters. Trees, ponds and stones block the way; stones break when you hit them. Hordes close in from time to time, and glowing elites hit harder but drop coins.
