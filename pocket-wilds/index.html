How to play

MouseClick the game to lock the mouse, then move it to turn the camera. W A S DMove. Forward is wherever the camera faces. SpaceUse your ultimate when the circle is full. Defeating monsters fills it. ECall a boss into the meadow whenever you feel ready. Bosses slam, charge and fire volleys, so watch for the red circle. MTurn sound on or off. EscPause and open the menu. Press again to resume. 1 2 3Pick a reward when you level up.

Your moves fire on their own. Walk over the glowing orbs to gain experience and pick a new move or trait each level. Your partner only learns moves of its own type. You heal slowly when you avoid damage for a few seconds.

Radar. The circle at the top right shows monsters (coloured by type), bosses (big red dots), crates (brown) and items (gold). The gold ring is the fence at the edge of the meadow.

Crates and drops. Break crates by attacking or walking into them. Crates and some monsters drop a Heal Berry, Magnet (pulls in every orb), Bomb (blasts everything nearby), Shield (6 seconds of safety) or Star Candy (an instant level).

Your partner evolves at level 7 and level 15. A boss shows up every 2:30.

Recruits. Every wild monster you defeat counts toward recruiting its species, across all your runs. Reach the target and it joins you as a playable partner, even in the middle of a run. Bosses join after you beat them once. Trainer outfits unlock by surviving 5:00, beating a boss and winning a run.

    Choose trainer sheets such as PLAYER RED.png and PLAYER LEAF.png (16px cells; front, back and side walk frames along the top row of each of the four colour blocks). Your trainer walks with your partner. The sheets stay in this browser only.
